# Landing Section 04 R&D — Resolve to Create v0.1

**Status:** EXPERIMENTAL CODED DIRECTION  
**Parent:** `docs/ui/LANDING_BRAND_RD.md`  
**Base design evidence:** approved Section 01 Lab Matrix v0.3 + Section 02 One creative thread + Section 03 Living Library  
**Production boundary:** prototype only; production `/` remains untouched

## Purpose
Section 03 leaves the visitor inside a spatial field of durable Library media. The Landing should close that story without dropping into a generic marketing CTA card.

The concept is **Resolve to Create**: the active creative thread leaves the wider Library field, six visible media objects reduce to four core media cells, and those cells resolve into the exact modular proportions of the locked RenderLab `R`. The surrounding copy and actions stay calm and legible while the media does the expressive work.

The closing meaning is simple: work does not disappear; it becomes the material for what happens next.

## Inherited visual grammar
Preserve from the approved Landing work:
- exact locked RenderLab identity asset; no logo redraw or reinterpretation;
- dark restrained canvas and concise sans typography;
- media dominance over chrome;
- modular geometry derived from the locked `R` without repeating the logo as wallpaper;
- transformation before replacement;
- motion concentrated on meaningful media objects;
- deliberate narrow/mobile composition;
- complete reduced-motion state;
- invitation-only product truth and existing route targets.

## Desktop composition
- a concise left copy block contains the locked brand, final statement, access truth and two actions;
- a large right media field begins as a loose continuation of the Living Library spatial arrangement;
- four core media objects resolve into the exact four-module `R` geometry;
- two peripheral Library objects yield away and disappear as the active thread resolves;
- the finished media mosaic remains media-filled rather than pretending to replace the locked logo itself;
- the actual locked logo asset remains separately present in the copy block.

## Interaction choreography
1. **Library carry-over:** six media objects are visible; four are the active creative thread and two remain peripheral context.
2. **Resolve:** scroll moves the four active objects toward their final `R` module positions while rotation/depth settle.
3. **Yield:** the two peripheral Library objects move outward and recede.
4. **Settle:** the media cells finish in the locked `R` proportions and stop moving.
5. **Action:** `Open Create` and `Sign in` remain continuously available; the animation never gates navigation.
6. **Reverse:** scrolling back separates the media cells and restores the peripheral Library context.

The animation is a brand/storytelling transition only. It does not imply that the product literally rearranges Library objects into a logo.

## Narrow / touch
- no long sticky scroll sequence is required;
- show the already-resolved four-cell media `R` as a compact visual conclusion;
- keep the final statement and actions immediately reachable;
- stack actions with practical touch targets;
- no pointer-only meaning.

## Product truth
The final actions remain exactly:
- **Open Create** → `/create`
- **Sign in** → `/settings`

Keep `Closed beta · invitation only` / no-public-sign-up truth visible. Do not add public registration, waitlist, pricing, testimonials, metrics, provider/model claims or unsupported capability promises.

## Media

A production Landing should replace them with owned/cleared RenderLab media while preserving the accepted geometry, density and transformation intent if this section is approved.
