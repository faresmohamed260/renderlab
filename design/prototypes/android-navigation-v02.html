RenderLab Android — v0.2 navigation review

Interactive design-only overlay on the static SVG frames. No app, backend, login, real media or data writes.

Library Feed

Private-media Feed is a root destination. Browse is a second view of the same Library.

Navigation model: Feed and Browse are root Library views and keep the five-icon dock. Opening a specific Collection creates a contextual Grid with back navigation and no dock. The source/filter sheet and media-action sheet are overlays, not separate product destinations.
  1. Click or keyboard-activate Browse in the bottom bar.
  2. Click Collections to open a context-only collection Grid. Use Back or Esc to return to Browse.
  3. Use Browse overflow to inspect filtering and Upload discovery.
  4. Use Feed asset overflow for labeled creative actions.

Selection and media actions are visual proposals only. Create, Activity, Settings, Viewer, Upload and real filters have not been implemented here. Actual RenderLab mobile SDK58 touch, TalkBack and OS Back acceptance remains separate.
