# Lab Grid / modular R — vector draft notes

**Status:** R&D geometry draft; not production artwork  
**Task:** #151

The selected brand concept is the modular `R` approved by the user during Brand Identity R&D 0.1.

This draft intentionally proves the mark in monochrome before palette, depth, glow, or motion are applied.

## Current construction
The v0.1 mark uses four visually separate modules:
- upper-left render/lab cell;
- rounded upper-right bowl module;
- lower-left render/lab cell;
- lower-right leg module.

Together they resolve immediately into `R`, while the visible separation preserves the idea of discrete render blocks / experiment cells assembling into a finished identity.

## What v0.1 proves
- recognizable `R` silhouette without color;
- strong light-on-dark and dark-on-light behavior;
- legibility down to the 16px pressure-test context;
- compact geometry suitable for an app rail / favicon direction;
- no dependence on animation, bloom, or gradient for recognition.

## What remains open
- optical tuning of the gap widths at 16–24px;
- exact bowl radius and inner counter geometry;
- leg angle / terminal balance;
- clearspace rule;
- final wordmark spacing and weight;
- final palette;
- optional motion sequence showing separated modules resolving into the mark;
- final cross-surface approval.
